how the engine works
01 / SELECT

Pick by strategy

Choose a key by priority, round-robin or weighted — always skipping keys in cooldown or over budget.

02 / DETECT

Classify failure

401/403 disables the key and flags it in admin. 429/5sx starts a 60s cooldown; the request retries on the next key mid-stream.

03 / RECOVER

Self-heal

Expired cooldowns rejoin the pool automatically. Monthly budget caps are the final guard — exhausted keys are skipped until reset.
